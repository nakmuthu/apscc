About APSCC

For a future that works with nature.

APSCC is a not-for-profit organisation advancing sustainability across campuses and communities through research, collaboration, education and practical action.

Explore our workMeet our leadership

“As members of the present generation, we hold the Earth in full trust for future generations.”

Our purpose

Global ambition.
Grounded action.

We help educational institutions, communities and partners translate sustainability commitments into practice—where people learn, live and work.

ResearchAction-oriented inquiry and peer-reviewed publications—including the edited volume Higher Education & SDG 15: Life on Land (Emerald, 2026)—translating knowledge into practice.
PolicyGlobal frameworks thoughtfully contextualised for India’s social and environmental realities.
PracticeCapacity building, conservation projects and collaborative programmes that can take root locally.
What guides us

Sustainability is a shared practice.

We view sustainability as social, economic, ecological and environmental at once. Our work is guided by intergenerational equity, precaution, diversity, flexibility and partnership.

InterdependenceHealthy campuses and communities depend on healthy ecosystems—and on one another.
PreventionAct early and responsibly to prevent environmental degradation, even when uncertainty remains.
ParticipationBuild the skills, confidence and local ownership that allow change to endure.
Global engagement

Local practice, connected to global conversations.

APSCC contributes to the global environmental dialogue through a network of 15+ United Nations and international partners — from UN ECOSOC, UNEP and the FAO Global Soil Partnership to the World Water Quality Alliance, ULSF (Talloires Declaration) and the International Association of Universities.
