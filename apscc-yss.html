Global Engagement · Young Soil Scientist

Healthy soils, healthy futures.

The Young Soil Scientist programme nurtures the next generation of soil stewards — because healthy soils are the living foundation of food security, biodiversity and climate resilience.

Why soil matters

The living foundation of life.

Agriculture is the science, art and practice of cultivating plants and livestock — and healthy soils are the basis for healthy food production, the medium in which nearly all food-producing plants grow to nourish humans, wildlife and livestock.

Soils supply the essential nutrients, water and oxygen that plants need to flourish, and buffer delicate roots from drastic temperature fluctuations. A healthy soil is a living, dynamic ecosystem — cycling nutrients, controlling pests, improving structure, and mitigating climate change by storing carbon.

"It is estimated that 95% of our food is directly or indirectly produced on our soils, and soil quality is directly linked to food quality and quantity."— FAO
