Activities & Events

Every initiative APSCC has undertaken — from local campus workshops to global policy participation.

Conference2013

Growth vs. Sustainability: Dr. Golda Edwin at the ISCN 2013 Conference, Singapore

Dr. Golda Edwin of APSCC addressed the 7th Annual ISCN Conference at the National University of Singapore — presenting on what a sustainable campus looks like in a developing country in the plenary “Growth vs. Sustainability: Are These Mutually Exclusive Goals?”

 Education2017

Science Beyond the Classroom: Summer Vacation Science Camp 2017

APSCC served as resource persons at the month-long Summer Vacation Science Camp at Dr. Abdul Kalam Science Centre, inspiring 100+ students to explore sustainability and environmental science.

 Conference2015

Building a Sustainable Chennai: APSCC at the IIT Madras Sustainability Summit 2015

APSCC collaborated with IIT Madras S-NET to host the Sustainability Summit 2015 — a panel discussion on sustainable urban design for Chennai and a case study challenge on solid waste management, bringing together experts and student innovators.

 Conference2013

Campus Challenge 2013: VIT University Vellore Receives APSCC's Green Campus Award

VIT University, Vellore received the Campus Challenge 2013 Award from APSCC at the Green Campus Summit 2013 in Puducherry — presented by Chief Minister N. Rangasamy to Vice-President Sankar Viswanathan.

Conference2014

Green Vision 2020: APSCC Title Sponsors S-NET Case Study Competition at IIT Madras 2014

APSCC partnered with IIT Madras as title sponsor for S-NET 2014, a case study competition challenging student teams to develop a 6-year 'Green Vision 2020' sustainable development plan for the fictional city of Vilaspur.

 Education2017

Living Archive: APSCC Students Visit the French Institute of Pondicherry Herbarium

APSCC organised a study visit to the Herbarium of the French Institute of Pondicherry — a botanical treasure housing 24,000+ specimens from 4,659 species, established in 1956 by Pierre Legris.

 Conservation2018

Kanagan Lake: 22 Threats, One Green Protocol

APSCC, with Dr. Golda Edwin and Dr. Nandhivarman, assessed Kanagan Lake at Oulgaret Municipality, identifying 22 urgent site-specific threats and recommending a Green Protocol for restoration, biodiversity, and eco-tourism.

 Conservation2018

Bahour Lake: Saving Puducherry's Second-Largest Freshwater Lake

APSCC and Pondicherry University assessed 26 site-specific threats to Bahour Lake — an IBA home to 25,000+ waterfowl — and developed a Green Protocol for sustainable lake management.

 Program2018

A Milestone for Sustainable Development: The Comprehensive Green Protocol for Puducherry

The Comprehensive Green Protocol (CGP) — developed by APSCC, Pondicherry University, PPCC and DST&E — was launched as a practical framework for educational institutions, industries, residences and organizations across Puducherry to adopt sustainable practices.

 Research2013

Water Through Native Roots: APSCC's Constructed Wetlands Research After UNESCO-IHE Training

Following training at UNESCO-IHE (the world's largest international graduate water education facility, Delft), APSCC researchers conducted peer-reviewed research demonstrating that native Arundo species matches Typha in constructed wetland water treatment — opening affordable local solutions for communities.

 Conference2013

On the National Mall: APSCC at the US EPA's 9th National Sustainable Design Expo

APSCC joined the US EPA's 9th Annual National Sustainable Design Expo on the National Mall in Washington D.C. \u2014 showcasing India's sustainability initiatives alongside 400 students, scientists, and engineers from across the globe.

Program2015

Model Green Campus: APSCC Founders Honoured by Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Alumni for Green Campus Initiative

JANVAA honoured APSCC's founders for transforming Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya into a model global sustainability campus — featuring biogas, vermicomposting, xeriscaping, and organic gardens that inspired visits from experts across 4 continents.

 Conference2012

Rio+20 and Beyond: APSCC at the World Symposium on Sustainable Development at Universities

APSCC participated in the World Symposium on Sustainable Development at Universities — a parallel event to Rio+20 — presenting on sustainability literacy in universities and the role of higher education in achieving the SDGs.

 Conservation2018

Oussudu Lake: APSCC's Vulnerability Assessment of Puducherry's First Bird Sanctuary

APSCC collaborated with Pondicherry University and Lt. Governor Kiran Bedi's office to conduct a comprehensive vulnerability assessment of Oussudu Lake — identifying 39 threats and recommending eco-tourism and conservation pathways.

 Education2017

10,000 Participants, One Mission: SLAPSWM 2017 Transforms Waste Management in Puducherry

The State-Level Awareness Program on Sustainable Waste Management (SLAPSWM 2017–18) brought together 10,000 participants across 90+ sessions spanning Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam — launching the Comprehensive Green Protocol.

 Conservation2017

Art for the Earth: Winners of the Climate Change & Biodiversity Drawing Contest, 4th Indian Biodiversity Congress

APSCC celebrated the winners of a drawing contest themed ‘Climate Change and Biodiversity’ held in conjunction with the 4th Indian Biodiversity Congress — inspiring young artists to visualise the connections between climate and nature.

 Conference2012

Water, Land & Food: APSCC at the International Summer School on Sustainable Territory Management, Todi, Italy

Dr. Edwin represented APSCC at the International Summer School on Sustainable Management and Promotion of Territory in Todi, Italy — sharing frameworks for bridging water availability with food security in vulnerable regions.

 Conference2013

The Green Campus Summit 2013: APSCC Hosts India's First International Summit on Campus Sustainability

APSCC organised the landmark Green Campus Summit 2013 in Puducherry — the first international summit of its kind in India — uniting 200+ delegates from 12 countries to advance campus sustainability across industrialised and developing regions.

 Partnership2016

Building Soil Resilience: APSCC Partners with the FAO Global Soil Partnership

APSCC partnered with the FAO Global Soil Partnership — committing to soil health, organic farming, and local food systems as the foundation of sustainable food security for campuses and communities.

 Partnership2013

Measuring What Matters: APSCC Becomes India's Regional Expert for the Sulitest

APSCC became the Regional or National Expert Committee (RNEC) for India for the Sulitest — the world's first international tool for assessing and enhancing sustainability literacy, created in the wake of Rio+20.

 Conference2020

Bridging Disciplines for a Sustainable Future

APSCC co-organised ICIAEESD 2020 in Puducherry — an interdisciplinary platform for academics, policymakers and practitioners advancing SDG-aligned sustainability research.

 Conservation2021

Answering Nature’s Call: APSCC Partners with The Green Institute for World Environment Day 2021

APSCC partnered with The Green Institute for a virtual symposium themed ‘Nature is Calling’ on World Environment Day 2021 — bringing together 25+ global sustainability leaders to discuss ecosystem restoration.

 Program2019

A Government Office Goes Green

APSCC partnered with the Chief Secretariat of Puducherry to implement 40+ green measures — from LED retrofits and rainwater harvesting to air-purifying plant networks — a model for sustainable government offices.

 Education2020

Green Protocol in Action: Capacity-Building with Sorbonne, Pondicherry University & JNV

APSCC, with Université Panthéon-Sorbonne (Paris), Pondicherry University and Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, ran a transformative training programme on the Green Protocol — regenerative agriculture, resource recovery and green entrepreneurship.

 Conservation2020

Uniting for Wildlife: The Wildlife Protection & Conservation Network

APSCC, Pondicherry University and Government of Puducherry departments jointly launched a network targeting nine species of concern — including the Olive Ridley Turtle, Peacock and Pondicherry Shark.

 Education2019

Health, Environment & the SDGs: Seminar on Good Health and Well-Being

APSCC organised a seminar on SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being — with Pondicherry University and Durham University (UK), exploring the environment–health nexus.

 Program2019

NASA Clean Air Plants at the Chief Secretariat

APSCC partnered with the Chief Secretariat of Puducherry to introduce air-purifying plants — backed by NASA’s Clean Air Study — under the Comprehensive Green Protocol, tackling VOCs, benzene and formaldehyde in government offices.

 Conservation2019

One Bag at a Time: Cotton Bag Drive at Kamban NSS Camp

During a 7-day NSS camp at Kamban Government Higher Secondary School, APSCC promoted a Cotton Bag Drive — distributing reusable bags and engaging students in practical action against single-use plastic pollution.

 Conference2019

Waste to Energy: National Workshop on Bioelectrochemical Technologies

APSCC co-organised a three-day SERB-DST national workshop on Bioelectrochemical Systems at Pondicherry University — microbial fuel cells, wastewater treatment and resource recovery, with experts from IIT Roorkee, CSIR-IICT, IIT Palakkad and SVNIT Surat.

 Research2021

Universities as Climate Leaders

Dr. Golda A. Edwin and Dr. M. Nandhivarman contributed to a landmark 7-country Körber Foundation study on higher education leadership and governance for sustainability and climate change.

 Conservation2022

Green Protocol for Wetlands: A Roadmap for Conservation

APSCC and Pondicherry University unveiled a five-strategy roadmap for wetland conservation, backed by the Forest and Agriculture Departments of Puducherry.

 Conference2021

International Seminar on Eco-Conscientious Reflective Practices

APSCC and Bharathidasan Government College for Women co-hosted an international seminar uniting 200+ delegates to turn environmental awareness into actionable sustainability.

 Partnership2021

From ESG to SDG: APSCC at the Economic Times Sustainability Summit 2021

APSCC served as a supporting association at the Economic Times 'ESG to SDG: Connected Paths for a Better Future' summit — a landmark corporate sustainability event bridging ESG principles with the 2030 SDG agenda.

 Education2024–25

Topsoil Conservation Awareness Series 2024–25

Aligned with World Soil Day 2024's theme 'Measure, Monitor, Manage', a year-long series engaging students and farmers in sustainable soil stewardship.

 Conservation2026

APSCC at the WWQA Conference

 Education2026

Global Young Soil Scientist Program (GYSS)

 Research2023

APSCC Contributes to Springer's Encyclopedia of SDG 15: Life on Land

Dr. Golda A. Edwin and Dr. Nandhivarman Muthu contributed landmark chapters to Springer's Encyclopedia of the UN SDGs “Life on Land” series — advancing evidence-based land management and soil science.

 Program2025

Traditional Organic Farmers Festival 2.0

APSCC co-organised the festival at Pondicherry University — uniting farmers, students, researchers and entrepreneurs to advance organic agriculture and green livelihoods.

 Partnership2026

APSCC Keynote at UNDP Climate Action Academy, Bosnia & Herzegovina

 Conservation2025

Cleaning Kalapet: APSCC Joins India's Coastal Cleanup Day 2025

APSCC co-organised International Coastal Cleanup Day 2025 at Kalapet Beach, Puducherry, under India's Swachh Sagar, Surakshit Sagar campaign — collecting marine-litter data to inform national policy.
