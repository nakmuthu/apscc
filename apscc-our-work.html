Activities & Events

Every initiative APSCC has undertaken — from local campus workshops to global policy participation.

Conference2020

Bridging Disciplines for a Sustainable Future

APSCC co-organised ICIAEESD 2020 in Puducherry — an interdisciplinary platform for academics, policymakers and practitioners advancing SDG-aligned sustainability research.

 Conservation2021

Answering Nature’s Call: APSCC Partners with The Green Institute for World Environment Day 2021

APSCC partnered with The Green Institute for a virtual symposium themed ‘Nature is Calling’ on World Environment Day 2021 — bringing together 25+ global sustainability leaders to discuss ecosystem restoration.

 Program2019

A Government Office Goes Green

APSCC partnered with the Chief Secretariat of Puducherry to implement 40+ green measures — from LED retrofits and rainwater harvesting to air-purifying plant networks — a model for sustainable government offices.

 Education2020

Green Protocol in Action: Capacity-Building with Sorbonne, Pondicherry University & JNV

APSCC, with Université Panthéon-Sorbonne (Paris), Pondicherry University and Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, ran a transformative training programme on the Green Protocol — regenerative agriculture, resource recovery and green entrepreneurship.

 Conservation2020

Uniting for Wildlife: The Wildlife Protection & Conservation Network

APSCC, Pondicherry University and Government of Puducherry departments jointly launched a network targeting nine species of concern — including the Olive Ridley Turtle, Peacock and Pondicherry Shark.

 Education2019

Health, Environment & the SDGs: Seminar on Good Health and Well-Being

APSCC organised a seminar on SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being — with Pondicherry University and Durham University (UK), exploring the environment–health nexus.

 Program2019

NASA Clean Air Plants at the Chief Secretariat

APSCC partnered with the Chief Secretariat of Puducherry to introduce air-purifying plants — backed by NASA’s Clean Air Study — under the Comprehensive Green Protocol, tackling VOCs, benzene and formaldehyde in government offices.

 Conservation2019

One Bag at a Time: Cotton Bag Drive at Kamban NSS Camp

During a 7-day NSS camp at Kamban Government Higher Secondary School, APSCC promoted a Cotton Bag Drive — distributing reusable bags and engaging students in practical action against single-use plastic pollution.

 Conference2019

Waste to Energy: National Workshop on Bioelectrochemical Technologies

APSCC co-organised a three-day SERB-DST national workshop on Bioelectrochemical Systems at Pondicherry University — microbial fuel cells, wastewater treatment and resource recovery, with experts from IIT Roorkee, CSIR-IICT, IIT Palakkad and SVNIT Surat.

 Research2021

Universities as Climate Leaders

Dr. Golda A. Edwin and Dr. M. Nandhivarman contributed to a landmark 7-country Körber Foundation study on higher education leadership and governance for sustainability and climate change.

 Conservation2022

Green Protocol for Wetlands: A Roadmap for Conservation

APSCC and Pondicherry University unveiled a five-strategy roadmap for wetland conservation, backed by the Forest and Agriculture Departments of Puducherry.

 Conference2021

International Seminar on Eco-Conscientious Reflective Practices

APSCC and Bharathidasan Government College for Women co-hosted an international seminar uniting 200+ delegates to turn environmental awareness into actionable sustainability.

 Partnership2021

From ESG to SDG: APSCC at the Economic Times Sustainability Summit 2021

APSCC served as a supporting association at the Economic Times 'ESG to SDG: Connected Paths for a Better Future' summit — a landmark corporate sustainability event bridging ESG principles with the 2030 SDG agenda.

 Education2024–25

Topsoil Conservation Awareness Series 2024–25

Aligned with World Soil Day 2024's theme 'Measure, Monitor, Manage', a year-long series engaging students and farmers in sustainable soil stewardship.

 Conservation2026

APSCC at the WWQA Conference

Dr. Nandhivarman Muthu represented APSCC at the UNEP-organised World Water Quality Alliance Conference in Nairobi, Kenya, connecting grassroots water action to global policy.

 Education2026

 Research2023

APSCC Contributes to Springer's Encyclopedia of SDG 15: Life on Land

 Program2025

 Partnership2026
