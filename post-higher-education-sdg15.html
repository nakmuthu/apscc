Higher Education and SDG 15: Life on Land

Edited by APSCC’s Golda A. Edwin — positioning universities as catalysts for land restoration and biodiversity conservation. Published by Emerald.

View on Emerald Bookstore ↗

Despite growing awareness of environmental degradation and biodiversity loss, many Higher Education Institutions remain insufficiently engaged in ecosystem-based solutions. This book closes that gap.

It provides evidence-based strategies, curricular innovations and case studies that illustrate how HEIs can embed SDG 15 into institutional priorities — advancing delivery by tackling environmental degradation and deforestation, and supporting land restoration and biodiversity conservation through teaching, research and community partnerships.

Global contributions, local action

With contributions spanning continents and contexts, each chapter maps to SDG 15 indicators and illustrates how HEIs are driving localized action with global impact:

NorwayEcological literacy in business education
ArgentinaReimagining agronomy curricula
SwitzerlandMulti-stakeholder collaboration
KenyaBiodiversity-focused research

Transcending siloed green initiatives, the book emphasizes institutional transformation through curriculum redesign, faculty development and multi-actor collaboration — essential reading for scholars, university leaders, curriculum designers and policy advocates.

Part of a 17-book series

Higher Education and the Sustainable Development Goals is a series of 17 books addressing each SDG through the lens of higher education, with a solutions-based approach to advancing Agenda 2030. Series edited by Wendy Purcell — Professor at Rutgers University and Academic Research Scholar at Harvard University; Emeritus Professor and University President Emerita.
