Multilateral Engagement

Translating Global Commitments into Grassroots Action

APSCC serves as a vital bridge between international environmental governance and local implementation. Through our consultative accreditations with United Nations bodies and strategic partnerships with global academic alliances, we empower universities, municipalities, and communities to implement practical, nature-positive solutions that address the triple planetary crisis.

Our Global Partners & Collaborations

Discover our multi-stakeholder partnerships spanning United Nations bodies, international higher education alliances, and regional sustainability networks.

UN Consultative Global

United Nations ECOSOC

New York, USA · United Nations
Special Consultative Status

Official consultative status granting APSCC direct participation in UN Economic and Social Council deliberations, High-Level Political Forums, and multilateral policy summits.

UN Environment Global

UNEP

Nairobi, Kenya · Global Secretariat
Accredited NGO & Assembly Observer

Accredited to the United Nations Environment Assembly (UNEA) with official observer status, contributing science-based recommendations to international environmental governance.

Land & Soil Global

UNCCD

Bonn, Germany · UN Secretariat
Accredited Observer Status

Active civil society observer advancing land degradation neutrality, sustainable topsoil management, and community drought resilience programs.

Biodiversity Global

IPBES

Bonn, Germany · Science-Policy Platform
Capacity-Building Task Force & ONet Member

Represented on the IPBES Capacity-Building Task Force (Plenaries 10–13) and Open-Ended Stakeholder Network (ONet) for global biodiversity conservation.

Plastics Treaty Global

UN Plastics INC

Global UN Treaty Committee
Authorized Observer

Observer participation in negotiating an international legally binding instrument on plastic pollution across aquatic, terrestrial, and urban environments.

Chemicals & Waste Global

UN Science-Policy Panel (SPP)

Geneva / Nairobi · UN Environment
Ad Hoc Working Group Observer

Engaged in the ad hoc open-ended working group establishing a global science-policy panel for chemicals, waste management, and pollution prevention.

Flagship Challenge Global 2021-30

UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration

UNEP & FAO Joint Secretariat
Global Challenge Lead Focal Point

Lead focal point steering the Cities Challenge (restoring urban spaces in 1,000+ cities by 2030) and Youth Challenge (10,000 green schoolyards).

Education Global

UNESCO Greening Education

Paris, France · UNESCO HQ
Core Team & Partner Organization

Partnering across Greening Schools, Greening Curriculum, Greening Teacher Training, and Greening Communities pillars to transform climate education.

Soil & Food Global

FAO Global Soil Partnership

Rome, Italy · FAO HQ
National Level Promoter & Partner

Promoting topsoil restoration, organic farming practices, and soil health monitoring through the FAO Global Soil Doctors Program.

Climate Adaptation Bonn / Global

UNFCCC Nairobi Work Programme

Bonn, Germany · Climate Secretariat
National Coordinator & Partner

Collaborating on climate vulnerability assessments, adaptation knowledge exchange, and resource efficiency models under the Nairobi Work Programme (NWP).

Freshwater Switzerland / Kenya

World Water Quality Alliance

UNEP & Swiss Confederation
Elected Advisory Committee Member

Expert committee membership providing global guidance on water quality assessments, freshwater ecosystem health, and aquifer stewardship.

Atmospheric Methane Global

UNEP IMEO Observatory

UNEP Methane Observatory Secretariat
Advisory Council / Mirror Group Member

Advising on methane emissions tracking, satellite data verification, and atmospheric reduction pathways across organic waste and agricultural systems.

University Leadership USA / Global

ULSF & Talloires Declaration

Washington D.C., USA
National Coordinator & Signatory

Coordinating higher education institutions under the Talloires Declaration framework administered by ULSF to embed sustainability in campus governance.

Higher Ed Network France / UNESCO

International Association of Universities

UNESCO House · Paris, France
Global Institutional Partner

Collaborating on Higher Education for Sustainable Development (HESD), climate action integration, and university sustainability benchmarking.

Tertiary Education United Kingdom

EAUC - The Sustainability Alliance

Cheltenham, United Kingdom
International Partner Organization

Partnering on tertiary education sustainability leadership, Green Gown Awards collaboration, and campus climate action planning tools.

Sustainability Literacy France / Global

Marseille, France
National Level Coordinator

Deploying global sustainability literacy assessments to evaluate environmental knowledge and SDG awareness among students and staff.

Green Office Germany

Cleantech Startups India / Global
