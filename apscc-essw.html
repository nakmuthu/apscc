UN Decade Challenge · ESSW

Environmental Stewardship & Sustainability Workshop.

An engaging, transformative workshop nurturing environmental responsibility in corporate and academic arenas — empowering professionals with the knowledge, tools and strategies to create a greener future.

The workshop

Nurturing environmental responsibility.

An engaging, gamified workshop empowering professionals and students from corporate and academic backgrounds with the knowledge, tools and strategies to drive positive change.

Delivered in half-day or full-day formats — virtual, in person or hybrid.

Empowering mission

Catalysts for positive change.

Presented by APSCC, this interactive event empowers students and professionals from academic and corporate backgrounds with the knowledge, tools and strategies to drive positive change and create a greener future.

Highly interactive & gamified. Available as half-day or full-day sessions, delivered virtually, in person or hybrid — and led by Dr. Nandhivarman Muthu and Dr. Golda A. Edwin, Global Leads for the UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration Cities & Youth Challenge.

Three delivery formats

Customized for every arena.

Corporates

Tailored sustainability training for corporate teams and leadership.

Universities & Colleges

Faculty and student workshops embedding sustainability in academia.

Schools

Age-appropriate, gamified environmental learning for younger students.
